Set up a salon or service business

For salons, nail studios, spas, barbers and other shops where customers book a service. About 20 minutes.

1
Register your business
  • On the home page click List your business (open).
  • Choose a username and password and enter your business name. You become the owner.
  • If you are logged in as a customer, log out first.
2
Add your branch
  • In the Business Workspace click Add a branch.
  • Pick the Type of business (for example Salon), then fill in address, area, city, PIN code, phone and opening / closing time.
  • Staff serving customers at the same time - used to show customers the waiting time.
  • Add nearby landmarks (e.g. "Near City Mall") so customers can find you in search.
3
Add services and rates
  • Open Services & rates. Pick a service name from the list or type a new one.
  • Enter the price and the average time in minutes. Untick Active to hide a service.
4
GST settings (if you are GST registered)
  • Open GST. Enter your GSTIN and state, and an invoice prefix (e.g. DST).
  • Choose your SAC code. For salons and beauty services GST is 5% without input tax credit by law, so the rate is set for you.
  • Not registered? Skip this step - bills are made without GST.
5
Wait for approval

QuickQueue checks new branches. Till then the branch shows Waiting for approval and customers cannot see it. You can keep setting up meanwhile.

6
Make your website
  • Open Website. Add tagline, about text, logo and banner photo, and pick a colour theme.
  • Add offers with start and end dates - the discount is applied on bills automatically.
  • Add gallery photos and your own sections (title, text and photo).
  • Tick Publish my website, then copy the link or download the QR to share.
7
Print the QR poster

Open QR poster and print it. Customers scan it at your door to join the queue or book.

8
Run the day
  • Open Today's queue. Open the branch in the morning and close it at night.
  • Click Start and End for each customer, or mark a no-show. The GST invoice is made for you.
9
Back office (optional, owner and branch admin)
  • Stock & purchases - add items and suppliers, and enter purchase bills.
  • Stock count - at month end staff count stock; you approve it.
  • Expenses - rent, salary, electricity and so on.
  • Profit & loss - pick a period and click Generate.
Staff logins: ask each person to register, choose Branch staff and pick your branch. Then approve them under Staff in the workspace.
