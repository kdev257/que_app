Set up a restaurant or dhaba

The setup is the same for a city restaurant, cafe or highway dhaba. Only how customers find you is different (see step 2).

1
Register your business
  • On the home page click List your business (open).
  • Choose a username and password and enter your business name. You become the owner.
2
Add your branch
  • In the Business Workspace click Add a branch and choose Restaurant as the type of business (also for a dhaba).
  • Fill in address, city, PIN code, phone and opening / closing time. Tick Offers home delivery if you deliver.
City restaurant
Customers search by city, area, PIN code or landmark. Add nearby landmarks (e.g. "Opp. Metro Station").
Highway dhaba
Travellers search by highway and KM stone. Open Highway in the workspace. Pick the highway (or add a new one), enter the KM stone at your dhaba and which side of the road travellers can reach you from.
3
Add your menu
  • Open Menu. Type a menu section (e.g. Starters, Thali) and an item name.
  • Enter price, preparation time and veg / non-veg. Untick Available when an item runs out.
4
Add tables and print table QR codes
  • Open Tables & QR. Add each table with its number and seats.
  • Print the table QR cards and keep one on each table. Guests scan and order without waiting for a waiter.
  • Guests choose to pay online now or pay after eating. Orders from the table are accepted automatically.
5
GST settings (if you are GST registered)
  • Open GST. Enter GSTIN, state, invoice prefix and SAC code.
  • Restaurants charge 5% without input tax credit. Tick Restaurant in a hotel only if the hotel had a room above ₹7,500 a day - then it is 18% with credit.
6
Wait for approval

QuickQueue checks new branches. Till then the branch shows Waiting for approval and customers cannot find it.

7
Make your website
  • Open Website: tagline, about, logo, banner, gallery, offers and your own sections.
  • Tick Publish my website and share the link or QR.
8
Run the day
  • Kitchen screen: accept or reject orders and mark them preparing and ready.
  • Staff / waiter screen: mark guests arrived, food served and order completed (cash collected).
9
Back office (optional, owner and branch admin)
  • Stock & purchases - groceries, gas, packing items and your suppliers.
  • Stock count at month end, Expenses, and Profit & loss for any period.
Staff logins (kitchen and waiters): ask each person to register, choose Branch staff and pick your branch. Then approve them under Staff in the workspace.
