Help for customers

Book a service or order food without standing in a line.

1
Login, register or continue as guest
  • You can look at shops and menus without logging in.
  • When you add to cart, log in, register or continue as guest.
  • Prices are shown after you log in or continue as guest.
2
Find a salon or service
  • Go to Services and pick a category.
  • Shops near your PIN code come first. You can also search by name, area or landmark.
3
Find a restaurant or dhaba
In the city
Open Find a restaurant and search by city, PIN code or landmark.
On a highway
Choose Highway search, pick the highway, enter the KM stone you are at, your direction and how far ahead to look. Dhabas on your way are listed.
4
Book a service
  • Add services to the cart, pick a time and confirm. Pay online or pay at the shop.
  • Track your token live and arrive when your turn is near. Offers are applied on the bill automatically.
5
Order from your table
  • Scan the QR code on the table with your phone camera.
  • Add dishes and place the order - no need to wait for a waiter.
  • Choose pay now online or pay after eating. Watch the order status on your phone.
6
Bill and review

You get a proper bill (GST invoice if the shop is registered). After your visit, rate the shop to help others.

For your safety you are logged out after 5 minutes of no activity (not while a booking is active).
