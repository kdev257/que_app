Why QuickQueue

What makes it different from a simple queue or billing app.

One app, not five

Customer side and back office in one place. A booking becomes a bill, the bill flows into your profit.

GST rules built in

Place of supply, fixed rates by law and GSTIN checks are done for you. Fewer mistakes at filing time.

Made for many branches

Owner, branch admin and staff each see only what they need. View one branch or all together.

Your data stays yours

Your sales, rates and customers are never shared with other shops.

Works on any phone

Runs in the browser and can be added to the home screen. No costly hardware.

Offers that apply themselves

Set an offer with dates once. The discount is applied on every bill automatically.

Supplier directory

Find suppliers listed by other businesses of your type in your city.

Ready for accounting

Every sale, purchase and expense carries an account code, so moving to full accounts is easy.

We do not track your location

QuickQueue never asks for your phone's GPS and never follows where you go. Shops near you are found from the PIN code or area you type - nothing more. More privacy for customers and businesses.
